Somnoimancer Null Set

I sing in tongues,
Psalms of mourning from a fallen god
Close your eyes
Let me kiss your dreams and make them bloom

Cover us,
holy one,
in the light
of your sun.

Purified
in the flood,
in the fire
of your love.

Why have you forsaken us?
Mother of this sacred dream that burns in us,
will you hear our voices, make us glorious?
